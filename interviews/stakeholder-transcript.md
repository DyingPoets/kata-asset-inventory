# Stakeholder Interview — IT Operations Manager
**Interviewer:** PDLC Practitioner
**Subject:** Jordan (IT Ops Manager, 6 years at company)
**Date:** [Session date]

---

**Q: Walk me through how you track assets today.**

"We have a spreadsheet. It lives in SharePoint. Three of us edit it. Last week someone deleted the formula column and we didn't notice for two days. We have laptops listed twice, some with different serial numbers — I don't know which is right. Contractors show up in the list mixed with employees."

**Q: What breaks when the data is wrong?**

"Audit time. We had an audit six months ago and we spent three days just cleaning the data before we could submit. The auditor flagged us for 'inadequate controls' which honestly... fair. Also when someone leaves, IT is supposed to reclaim their laptop. We've lost four laptops in the last year because nobody flagged them for collection."

**Q: Who else uses this data?**

"Finance uses it for depreciation. HR checks it when someone joins or leaves. Security runs reports from it monthly. None of them have write access — they just get exports."

**Q: If this was fixed tomorrow, what would be different?**

"I'd know in real time what we have, who has it, and when it expires. I'd get an alert when an asset is unassigned for more than 30 days. Finance would stop emailing me for exports."

**Q: What would you NOT want us to build?**

"Don't build a full ITSM system. We don't need a ticketing system. We just need a clean, reliable inventory with a simple check-in/check-out. Keep it simple."
